Introduction/ Problem Description: 

For the final project, I wanted my focus to explore how to use data sets in understanding job placements and the relationship to those placement rates given different factors. For this project, the data set called “Predicting Student Placement Using Academic Performance, IQ, and Internship Experience” from Kaggle under a public domain license. The data set was designed to look at student placement outcomes based off the different factors; academic performance, the college attend, and their intelligence, and internship experience (Ahmed, 2021). 
Academia and job placement is currently at the forefront of higher education policy, which is critical as it may begin to be tied to federal funding. Many higher education institutions have a form of tracking and reporting job placement data for students, but for college programs that may be at risk of being cut, looking at different factors of intervention for student job placement could become critical.	 
The purpose of this R Package will serve as a resource for users to understand student job placement rates in a predictive model with the predictors of employability.  Understanding experiential learning engagement with students via internships, their academic performance via GPA, and other factors like their IQ and college attendance can help to make informed decisions. This will help academic institutions standardize this process with a simple tool for analysis.
The R Package will provide a standardized workflow with the focus being on cleaning the data, summarizing, visualizing and then modeling. The R Package will help to simplify tasks and then will include custom functions, visualizations, and S3 class which is not provided within the standard base R. The package will also help users interpret the logistic regression within the specific contexts of the student placement. 
The rest of this report will focus on moving through the Package and these processes.
